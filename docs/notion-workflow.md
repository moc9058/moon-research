# Notion Article Workflow

Notion hub: [moon-research](https://app.notion.com/p/3d169428c8618198a261c0f821b25f98)

Use one row in the **Articles** database for each paper or article.

## Fields

| Field | What to store |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The article or paper title |
| File | The PDF or other article file |
| arXiv Link | The arXiv URL, when one exists |
| Summary | A brief explanation in your own words |
| Hypothesis | The question or claim the article tests |
| Key Idea | The main method, insight, or contribution |
| Limitation | Important weaknesses, assumptions, or open problems |

## Capture an article

1. Add a row and enter its title.
2. Upload the article to **File**.
3. Add its **arXiv Link** when available.
4. Fill the four note fields after reading.
5. Keep implementation code, tests, configurations, and results in GitHub.

Notion is the source of truth for the paper and reading notes. GitHub is the
source of truth for executable implementations and reproducibility evidence.
